## 1. Overview

**Research Analyst.** CRISPR Therapeutics develops gene-based medicines. Its portfolio covers hemoglobinopathies, cardiovascular disease, autoimmune disease, oncology, regenerative medicine, and rare diseases (earnings release, L118). CASGEVY is its first approved CRISPR-based therapy. Vertex leads global development, manufacturing, and commercialization. Vertex and CRSP share CASGEVY profits and program costs 60/40 (earnings release, L105-L106).

CRSP remains loss-making. For 2025, it reported $3.510 million of revenue, a $664.571 million operating loss, and a $581.599 million net loss (2025 10-K XBRL tags `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`, `OperatingIncomeLoss`, and `NetIncomeLoss`; period ended 2025-12-31; accession 0001193125-26-048957).

## 2. Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

**Research Analyst.** CASGEVY generated $99 million of product revenue in Q3 2026. This was 30% above Q2 2026 and nearly six times Q3 2025 (earnings release, L8 and L23). This is CASGEVY product revenue, not CRSP's reported company revenue. CRSP's income statement reported $12.669 million of total revenue in Q3 2026 (earnings release, L132-L157).

The commercial position expanded because CASGEVY was approved for younger patients in the United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and the United States (earnings release, L9 and L25). More than 750 patients had initiated treatment worldwide (earnings release, L23).

The pipeline offers additional catalysts:

- The FDA gave CTX310 RMAT designations for severe hypertriglyceridemia and elevated LDL-C (earnings release, L38).
- Phase 1b CTX310 data were scheduled for November 9, 2026 (earnings release, L44).
- Phase 1 zugo-cel data in systemic sclerosis were scheduled for November 8, 2026 (earnings release, L58-L62).
- CRSP completed enrollment in the Phase 2 CTX611 trial (earnings release, L54).

These programs may create value. They still carry clinical and regulatory risk. The release warns that investigational therapies may not complete development or gain approval (earnings release, L120-L122).

## 4. Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

**Research Analyst.** The nearest stated events were the November 8 zugo-cel presentation and the November 9 CTX310 presentation (earnings release, L44 and L62). Investors should compare those data with the safety and efficacy claims in the later filings. CASGEVY treatment starts and reported product revenue should also be tracked. CRSP recognizes only its contractual economics from the Vertex collaboration, so CASGEVY product revenue must not be copied directly into CRSP revenue.

Liquidity is currently substantial, but the company is funding several clinical programs and reported a Q3 loss. The decision should therefore be updated when clinical data, cash use, collaboration economics, or regulatory status changes.

## 5. Investment Recommendation

**Investment Advisor: HOLD.** The deciding number is **$2.2766 billion of cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities at September 30, 2026** (earnings release, L83-L84). This liquidity supports a broad pipeline. However, the company reported a $111.437 million Q3 net loss and remains exposed to binary clinical and regulatory outcomes. A Buy needs evidence that commercial economics or pipeline value outweigh those risks. A Sell needs evidence that the liquidity and pipeline cannot support that value. The present filing evidence does not establish either threshold.
